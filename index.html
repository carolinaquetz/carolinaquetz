Frequently Asked Questions

I already speak some Portuguese. Will this still help me?

Yes. Many learners can already form sentences and communicate basic ideas, but still struggle to understand Brazilians when they speak naturally.

This ebook focuses specifically on listening practice and becoming more familiar with spoken Brazilian Portuguese.

Is this for complete beginners?

This material is best for learners who already know some basic Portuguese and want more listening and pronunciation practice before traveling to Brazil.

The phrases, dialogues, and explanations will be much easier to follow if you already have some familiarity with the language.

Will this help with pronunciation too?

Yes. You can use the audio recordings for pronunciation practice by listening and repeating the phrases out loud.

Do I need to memorize everything before my trip?

No. The goal isn't to memorize every phrase. The goal is to become more familiar with the sounds, words, and patterns you're likely to hear.

Is this focused on grammar?

No. This ebook focuses on listening practice rather than teaching Portuguese grammar.

Why are there two audio versions in the THEY SAY sections?

The slow version helps you hear the words and pronunciation more clearly. The natural-speed version helps you get used to everyday Brazilian Portuguese. Both are included in the same file.

Is this Brazilian Portuguese?

Yes. All audio recordings and dialogues use Brazilian Portuguese.

Are the audio recordings made with AI?

No. The voices are real voices: mine, my boyfriend's, and my father's. I wanted the material to give you exposure to real voices and different ways of hearing Brazilian Portuguese.

How do the audio recordings work?

Every phrase and dialogue contains a clickable audio link inside the PDF. Simply click the text to open the audio in a new tab while you study.

How are the audio files delivered?

The ebook already includes clickable audio links for all phrases and dialogues. You will also receive a separate folder containing all the audio files, organized by topic and section.

Can I use the ebook on my phone?

For the best experience, I recommend using a computer or tablet for the practice. At certain points, you'll need to listen to the audio while reading the Portuguese sentence at the same time.
